GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to help you feel prepared before you begin.

What skills does the assessment cover?

The assessment covers SQL and Python, with questions across easy, intermediate and advanced difficulty levels.

How many attempts do I have?

Your current assessment setting allows {{ cfg.max_attempts }} attempt{{ cfg.max_attempts|pluralize }}. Please review your answers before submitting.

How much time do I have?

{% if cfg.time_limit_minutes %}You have {{ cfg.time_limit_minutes }} minutes after starting an attempt. The timer is based on your assessment start time.{% else %}There is no time limit configured for the assessment.{% endif %}

Is there a penalty for a wrong answer?

No. There is no negative marking. Incorrect answers do not reduce the marks you earn for correct answers.

When will I see my result?

Your result is available after you submit your attempt. It includes your overall score, skill-wise scores and pass or fail status.

What information do you collect?

We use your name, email, phone number and assessment answers to operate the assessment and report results to ITA Associates. Access is limited to authorised administrators.
